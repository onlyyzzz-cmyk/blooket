v1.5 — Calculator Pro, Sign Up & Partners

Latest

September 19, 2026

  • Calculator upgrade: calculation history, Ans recall, DEG/RAD toggle, keyboard support, live answer preview, and a copy button.
  • New unit conversions: speed, volume, and time — alongside length, weight, temperature, and data.
  • Fixed the converter grid layout so all categories display correctly.
  • Added Sign up with secure account creation, plus sign-up buttons in the site and chat headers.
