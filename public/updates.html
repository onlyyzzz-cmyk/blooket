Update Log

Everything that ships to AITutor, newest first.

v1.6 — Community, Moderation & Staff Forms

Latest

September 19, 2026

  • Launched the Community — a shared chat where students can talk, ask questions, and help each other.
  • Moderators get a purple Mod badge and admins a red Admin badge on their messages.
  • Mods can mute members (1 minute to 7 days), ban or unban them from the site community, and delete any message.
  • Admins can additionally grant or revoke Moderator and Admin roles.
  • New Forms page: report a member who broke the rules, or apply for a Moderator / Admin position.
  • Staff applications now require a free account — sign up with Clerk to apply, and the result is tied to your sign-in.
  • An AI reviewer scores every application instantly and the decision (with feedback) is emailed to your account email.
  • Approved applicants get a Mod badge and moderation tools immediately when they sign in — mute, ban, and delete messages in the community.
  • New Report a bug form: our AI instantly triages your report by severity and category so fixes land faster.
  • Added a forms section to the home page — one-click access to bug reports, member reports, and staff applications.
  • Removed the experimental keep-alive self-ping that could keep sleeping servers from resting properly.

v1.5 — Calculator Pro, Sign Up & Partners

September 19, 2026

  • Calculator upgrade: calculation history, Ans recall, DEG/RAD toggle, keyboard support, live answer preview, and a copy button.
  • New unit conversions: speed, volume, and time — alongside length, weight, temperature, and data.
  • Fixed the converter grid layout so all categories display correctly.
  • Added Sign up with secure account creation, plus sign-up buttons in the site and chat headers.
  • Photos: if the selected model can't view images, the tutor now tells you to switch to Qwen 3.8 27B and re-send the photo.
  • Uploaded photos now stay visible in your chat message and history.
  • Fixed an error where sending a text message right after a photo could fail.
  • Partnerships: partner cards now link to partner sites, and the teams section has a join call-to-action.

v1.3 — Fair Use & Stability

September 2026

  • Added fair-use rate limiting (20 tutoring requests per minute) to keep the tutor fast for everyone.
  • Added a rolling cap of 200 saved chats and automatic cleanup of expired rate-limit entries.
  • Improved error messages when the AI provider is unreachable.
