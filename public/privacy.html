Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. The short version

AITutor has no accounts, no passwords, and no sign-up forms. We don't collect your name, email, or anything that identifies you personally. The main thing we store is the chat history you choose to save, and it stays on our server (and in your browser's local storage) so My Chats can show it back to you.

2. What we collect

  • Questions and homework photos you send. Used to generate your explanation and, if you save the chat, stored in chat history.
  • Chat history. If you save a chat, its messages and subject label are stored in chats.json on the server so you can reopen it. Saved chats are capped at 200 entries.
  • Model choice and subject filters. Stored in your browser's local storage, never on our servers.
  • Basic server logs. Our host may keep standard, short-lived technical logs (like IP addresses) for security and rate limiting. We apply fair-use limits per visitor to keep AITutor fast for everyone.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and there are no third-party analytics scripts on the site.

3. How AI providers are involved

When you ask a question, your prompt (and any photo you attach) is sent to Groq, our AI provider, to generate the explanation. Groq processes that content to produce your answer. See Groq's own privacy policy for how they handle data.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data — there's nothing to sell and no one to sell it to.
  • We don't show ads or embed third-party trackers.
  • We don't require accounts, so we don't build profiles of you.
  • We don't email you — we never ask for your email in the first place.

5. Shared devices

Because there are no accounts, saved chats are visible to anyone using the same browser on the same device. On shared or public computers, delete chats you don't want others to see (the ✕ button in My Chats) or browse in private/incognito mode.

6. Data retention and deletion

Saved chats live until you delete them, with a rolling cap of the 200 most recent. Deleting a chat removes it from the list immediately. If a host resets its filesystem, in-memory history may be lost — that's a limitation of file-based storage, not a privacy choice.

7. Children's privacy

AITutor is designed for students, including younger ones. Because we collect no personal information, we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK) — there's nothing collected to begin with. Parents and teachers: the only stored content is the study questions and images users choose to send.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and announce significant changes in the update log.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at partners@aitutor.app.
