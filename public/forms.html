Report a member who broke the rules, or apply to join the moderation team.

Sign up to apply

Staff applications are tied to your free AITutor account so the AI reviewer can email you the result and your mod tools stay linked to your sign-in. Create an account or sign in to continue.

How reviews work

Applications require a free account. When you submit, an AI reviewer scores your application and emails the result to your account email within a minute. Approved applicants get a Mod badge and immediate access to the moderation tools in the community when they sign in. Every report is stored and reviewed by the admin team.
